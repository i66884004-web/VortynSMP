THE SERVER

Vortyn SMP is a survival Minecraft server where players can build, explore, fight, trade, and create their own stories while following a clear set of competitive gameplay rules.

🌎

Survival

Build your base, gather resources, explore the world, and survive alongside the rest of the Vortyn community.

⚔️

Fair Combat

PvP is part of the server, with rules designed to keep fights competitive without unfair exploits or combat escapes.

👥

Team Up

Teams can have up to four players. Coordinate with your team, compete with other groups, and make your own path on Vortyn.

SERVER RULES

Follow the rules, respect the community, and keep Vortyn SMP fun for everyone. Discord rules apply to Minecraft too.

01

Respect & Common Sense

Keep the community respectful. Flaming, harassment, slurs, targeted hate speech, and persistent toxicity are not tolerated. Use common sense.

02

No Spam or Annoying Behavior

Do not spam text, emojis, caps, commands, or unnecessary mentions. Keep discussions in the appropriate channels.

03

No Advertising or Unapproved Self-Promotion

Do not advertise other Minecraft servers, Discord communities, or personal social media or streams outside designated areas. Advertising through DMs is prohibited.

04

Keep Content Appropriate

No NSFW, explicit, graphic, or illegal content anywhere on the Discord or Minecraft community. This includes avatars, statuses, banners, and nicknames.

05

Respect Staff

Follow staff directions. If you believe a staff decision was unfair, use a ticket or the appropriate private process instead of arguing publicly.

06

No Exploiting, Hacking, or Unfair Advantages

Do not use hacks, cheats, exploits, or other unfair advantages. Do not abuse plugins, rules, or admin powers.

07

No Danger or Combat Logging

Do not combat log or intentionally leave a dangerous situation to avoid its consequences. If you are kicked from something during combat, you must log back on.

08

No Water Stalling

Do not use water stalling to intentionally delay or avoid combat.

09

No Stasis Chambers

Stasis chambers are not allowed.

10

No Teleporting Away During Combat

Do not use commands or other teleportation methods to escape a fight.

11

No Alts

Alternative accounts are not allowed on either Minecraft or Discord.

12

No Explosives in Combat

Do not use explosives during combat, including explosive traps.

13

No Pearls in Combat

Do not use Ender Pearls during combat.

14

No Elytras, Tridents, or Spears to Escape

Do not use elytras, tridents, or spears to run away from an active fight.

15

Maximum Team Size: 4

Teams are limited to four players. Alliances between teams are not allowed.

16

Discord Rules Apply to Minecraft

The Discord rules also apply to player behavior in Minecraft and across the Vortyn SMP community.

⚠️ Moderation

Breaking the rules may result in a warning, timeout, kick, or ban, depending on the severity and frequency of the offense. Staff may take action against behavior that negatively affects the community, even if a specific situation is not listed word-for-word above.
